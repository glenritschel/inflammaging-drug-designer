Senotherapeutic Placeholder Drug Designer — Inflammaging

Design a hypothetical anti-aging-inflammation drug across the causal map. Two axes: which nodes it engages (senolytic = clear the senescent cell; senomorphic = silence the SASP), and where it is allowed to act (systemic, PROTAC degrader, or senescent-cell-targeted). The dose-limiting harms are on-target effects in the wrong cell; cell-selective delivery is the lever.

Efficacy weights and severities are illustrative design heuristics for exploring trade-offs, not measured pharmacology. Biology grounded in: senolytic/senomorphic reviews; navitoclax BCL-xL thrombocytopenia; galacto-conjugated (SA-β-gal-activated) and PROTAC senolytics for cell-selectivity; NF-κB/JAK/mTOR/NLRP3/cGAS-STING SASP modulation. "On-target / wrong cell" effects are removable by cell-selective delivery; effects arising in the senescent cell itself (e.g., loss of beneficial transient senescence) are removable only by dropping the target.
